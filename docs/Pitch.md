## 30-Second Elevator Pitch

Businesses don't just need more energy data—they need better energy decisions.

GridSense AI is an AI-powered Energy Decision Support Platform that helps SMEs predict future energy costs, identify inefficiencies, and receive personalized recommendations to reduce operational expenses.

## 2-Minute Pitch

Small and Medium-sized Enterprises are facing increasing energy costs, unreliable electricity supply, and difficult decisions about how to manage their energy expenses.

Most existing solutions only display historical data through dashboards. They tell businesses what happened yesterday, but they don't help them decide what to do tomorrow.

That's where GridSense AI comes in.

GridSense AI is an AI-powered Energy Decision Support Platform designed specifically for SMEs.

Businesses upload their historical energy data, and our platform uses Machine Learning to predict future energy costs and calculate an Energy Score.

We then use Generative AI to explain those predictions in simple language and recommend practical actions, complete with estimated savings, priority levels, and expected return on investment.

Rather than overwhelming users with charts, GridSense AI focuses on one question:

"What should the business do next?"

By turning complex energy data into actionable decisions, we help businesses reduce costs, improve efficiency, and move toward more sustainable operations.

## Why did you build this?

GridSense AI was inspired by the real challenges SMEs face in managing rising energy costs and making informed decisions with limited analytical tools.

---

## Why use AI?

Machine Learning predicts future trends, while Generative AI explains those predictions in clear, actionable language.

---

## What makes your solution different?

Most energy platforms focus on reporting historical data.

GridSense AI focuses on helping businesses make better future decisions.

---

## Who benefits?

Small and Medium-sized Enterprises, particularly businesses with significant energy expenses such as manufacturers, hotels, restaurants, schools, and healthcare facilities.

---

## How accurate are your predictions?

Predictions are based on historical energy records and evaluated using standard machine learning metrics. The platform also displays a confidence score so users understand the reliability of each prediction.

---

## Where did your dataset come from?

Our MVP uses a hybrid approach: publicly available energy datasets combined with carefully engineered SME-specific features and realistic synthetic records to model common business energy scenarios.

---

## Why did you choose this tech stack?

Next.js provides a fast and responsive frontend, FastAPI offers high-performance APIs, PostgreSQL ensures reliable data storage, Scikit-Learn supports efficient machine learning workflows, and Google Gemini helps translate predictions into understandable business recommendations.

---

## How would this scale?

The architecture is modular. We can add IoT devices, smart meter integrations, carbon tracking, multi-tenant organizations, and mobile applications without redesigning the core platform.
