# Development Roadmap

**Project:** GridSense AI

**Version:** 1.0.0

# Project Goal

Build an AI-powered Energy Decision Support Platform that enables SMEs to analyze energy consumption, predict future costs, receive AI-powered recommendations, and generate professional reports.
